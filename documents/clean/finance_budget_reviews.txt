Northstar Industries
Budget Reviews
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-033
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for budget reviews. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Department budgets are reviewed monthly.
2.2 The finance director signs off on quarterly budget forecasts.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses budget reviews. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
