Northstar Industries
Employee Onboarding
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-024
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for employee onboarding. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 New hires receive their induction schedule at least three business days before starting.
2.2 The hiring manager assigns an onboarding buddy.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses employee onboarding. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
