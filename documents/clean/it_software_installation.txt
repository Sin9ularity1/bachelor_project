Northstar Industries
Software Installation
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-003
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for software installation. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 The IT service desk must approve installation of software not in the approved catalog.
2.2 Unsupported software must be removed within five business days of detection.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses software installation. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
