Northstar Industries
Device Enrollment
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-002
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for device enrollment. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 New laptops must be enrolled in device management before being issued to employees.
2.2 The IT operations manager must approve any device-enrollment exception.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses device enrollment. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
