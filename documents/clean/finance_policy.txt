Northstar Industries
Finance Policy
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-029
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for finance policy. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Purchases exceeding 10,000 DKK require manager approval.
2.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses finance policy. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
