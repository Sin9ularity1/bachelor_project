Northstar Industries
IT Change Management
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-006
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for it change management. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Routine production changes require at least two business days notice.
2.2 Emergency production changes must be approved by the on-call IT lead.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses it change management. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
