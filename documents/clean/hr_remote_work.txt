Northstar Industries
Remote Work
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-022
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for remote work. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
2.2 The employee’s manager approves remote work arrangements.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses remote work. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
