Northstar Industries
IT Security Policy
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-001
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for it security policy. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 All employee passwords must contain at least 14 characters.
2.2 Multifactor authentication is mandatory for remote access.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses it security policy. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
