HR Policy
Document status: Approved

Document ID: NS-021
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for hr policy. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave.
2.2 The standard probation period is three months.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses hr policy. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
