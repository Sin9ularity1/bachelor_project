Northstar Industries
Remote Support
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-010
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for remote support. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 A technician must obtain the employee’s consent before starting a remote-control session.
2.2 Remote support session logs are kept for 90 days.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses remote support. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
