Northstar Industries
Network Access
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-009
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for network access. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Visitors must use the guest Wi-Fi network, not the corporate LAN.
2.2 Temporary wired network exceptions expire after seven days.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses network access. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
