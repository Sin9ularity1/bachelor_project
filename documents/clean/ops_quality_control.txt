Northstar Industries
Quality Control
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-041
Department: Operations
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for quality control. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Quality control checks five samples per production batch.
2.2 Only the quality manager may release a batch after documented retesting.

3. Responsibilities
The operations function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses quality control. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
