Northstar Industries
Document Control
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-046
Department: Management
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for document control. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Controlled procedures are reviewed annually.
2.2 The document controller assigns controlled document revision numbers.

3. Responsibilities
The management function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses document control. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
