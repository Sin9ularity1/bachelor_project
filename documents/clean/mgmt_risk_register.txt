Northstar Industries
Risk Register
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-048
Department: Management
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for risk register. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 High-priority risks are reviewed monthly.
2.2 The risk and compliance manager owns the corporate risk register.

3. Responsibilities
The management function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses risk register. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
