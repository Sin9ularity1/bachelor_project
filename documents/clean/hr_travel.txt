Northstar Industries
Business Travel
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-026
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for business travel. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 International business travel should be requested at least fourteen days in advance.
2.2 International business travel requires department-head approval.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses business travel. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
