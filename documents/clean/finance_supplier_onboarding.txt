Northstar Industries
Supplier Onboarding
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-035
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for supplier onboarding. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 New supplier approval requires verification of tax registration and bank details.
2.2 The accounts payable lead approves new supplier master records.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses supplier onboarding. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
