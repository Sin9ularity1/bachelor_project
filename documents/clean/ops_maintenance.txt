Northstar Industries
Preventive Maintenance
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-037
Department: Operations
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for preventive maintenance. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Production line inspections are scheduled every two weeks.
2.2 The maintenance supervisor signs completed maintenance checklists.

3. Responsibilities
The operations function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses preventive maintenance. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
