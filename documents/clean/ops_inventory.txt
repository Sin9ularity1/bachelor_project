Northstar Industries
Inventory Management
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-042
Department: Operations
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for inventory management. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Inventory cycle counts are performed weekly.
2.2 Stock adjustments above 2,000 DKK require warehouse-manager approval.

3. Responsibilities
The operations function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses inventory management. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
