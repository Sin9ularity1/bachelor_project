Northstar Industries
Privileged Access
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-014
Department: Security
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for privileged access. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Privileged access rights are reviewed every quarter.
2.2 Administrators must not use privileged accounts for everyday email.

3. Responsibilities
The security function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses privileged access. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
