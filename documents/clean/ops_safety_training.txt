Northstar Industries
Safety Training
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-039
Department: Operations
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for safety training. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Warehouse safety training must be renewed every twelve months.
2.2 The safety coordinator maintains warehouse safety training records.

3. Responsibilities
The operations function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses safety training. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
