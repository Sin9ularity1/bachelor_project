Northstar Industries
Expense Reimbursement
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-031
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for expense reimbursement. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Employee expense reimbursements are processed twice a month.
2.2 Expense reimbursement claims must include an itemized receipt.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses expense reimbursement. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
