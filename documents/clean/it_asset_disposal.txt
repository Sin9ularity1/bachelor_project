Northstar Industries
IT Asset Disposal
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-008
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for it asset disposal. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Storage drives must undergo verified cryptographic erasure before recycling.
2.2 The IT asset coordinator signs off on retired asset disposal.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses it asset disposal. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
