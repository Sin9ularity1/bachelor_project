Northstar Industries
Equipment Checkout
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-040
Department: Operations
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for equipment checkout. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 The maximum standard equipment loan period is fourteen days.
2.2 The equipment custodian approves equipment loan extensions.

3. Responsibilities
The operations function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses equipment checkout. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
