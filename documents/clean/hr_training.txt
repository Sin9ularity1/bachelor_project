Northstar Industries
Training and Development
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-025
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for training and development. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 The standard annual training budget is 6,000 DKK per employee.
2.2 Training expenses above the standard budget require department-head approval.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses training and development. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
