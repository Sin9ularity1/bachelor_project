Northstar Industries
Management Meetings
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-045
Department: Management
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for management meetings. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 The executive operations committee meets every Monday.
2.2 Meeting minutes are distributed within two business days.

3. Responsibilities
The management function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses management meetings. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
