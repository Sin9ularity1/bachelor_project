Northstar Industries
Purchase Orders
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-032
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for purchase orders. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 The procurement team creates purchase orders for approved purchases.
2.2 Purchase orders must be created before the supplier is engaged.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses purchase orders. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
