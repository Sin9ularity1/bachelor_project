Northstar Industries
Performance Reviews
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-027
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for performance reviews. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Formal performance reviews are held twice a year.
2.2 The direct manager documents performance review outcomes.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses performance reviews. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
