Northstar Industries
Sick Leave
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-023
Department: HR
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for sick leave. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Employees must report sickness absence before their scheduled start time.
2.2 The direct manager receives the initial sick-leave notification.

3. Responsibilities
The hr function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses sick leave. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
