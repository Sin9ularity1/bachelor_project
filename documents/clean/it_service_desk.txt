Northstar Industries
IT Service Desk
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-007
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for it service desk. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Critical incidents have a service desk response target of 30 minutes.
2.2 Employees must report nonurgent IT problems through the IT ticket portal.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses it service desk. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
