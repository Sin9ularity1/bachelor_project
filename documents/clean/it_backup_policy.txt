Northstar Industries
Backup Policy
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-004
Department: IT
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for backup policy. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Production file shares are backed up every night.
2.2 Daily file-share backups are retained for 30 days.

3. Responsibilities
The it function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses backup policy. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
