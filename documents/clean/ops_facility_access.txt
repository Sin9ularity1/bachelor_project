Northstar Industries
Facilities Access
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-038
Department: Operations
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for facilities access. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Main reception closes at 17:00 on weekdays.
2.2 Deliveries outside reception hours must be directed to the loading bay.

3. Responsibilities
The operations function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses facilities access. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
