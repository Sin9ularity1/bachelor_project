Northstar Industries
Project Governance
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-047
Department: Management
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for project governance. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Every project must have an approved project charter before work begins.
2.2 The project sponsor chairs project steering meetings.

3. Responsibilities
The management function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses project governance. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
