Northstar Industries
Invoice Processing
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-030
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for invoice processing. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Supplier invoices must include a purchase order number.
2.2 Supplier payment batches are processed every Thursday.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses invoice processing. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
