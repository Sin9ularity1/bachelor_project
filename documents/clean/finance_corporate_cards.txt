Northstar Industries
Corporate Cards
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-034
Department: Finance
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for corporate cards. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 The default corporate card single-transaction limit is 5,000 DKK.
2.2 Lost corporate cards must be reported immediately to finance.

3. Responsibilities
The finance function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses corporate cards. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
