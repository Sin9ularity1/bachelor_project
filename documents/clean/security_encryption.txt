Northstar Industries
Encryption Standard
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-017
Department: Security
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for encryption standard. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 All employee laptops must use full-disk encryption.
2.2 Encryption recovery keys must be stored in the approved key escrow service.

3. Responsibilities
The security function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses encryption standard. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
