Northstar Industries
Phishing Reporting
Document status: Approved
Classification: Internal
Document ID: NS-011
Department: Security
Version: 1.0

1. Purpose
This procedure establishes the Northstar Industries requirements for phishing reporting. It applies to employees and relevant approved contractors.

2. Requirements
2.1 Employees must forward suspected phishing messages to the security reporting mailbox.
2.2 Employees must not click links or open attachments in suspected phishing messages.

3. Responsibilities
The security function maintains this document and records exceptions through the normal approval workflow. Employees must follow the requirements stated above.

4. Related guidance
This document addresses phishing reporting. Other Northstar Industries procedures may cover related but distinct workflows. In the event of uncertainty, employees should consult the responsible department.
